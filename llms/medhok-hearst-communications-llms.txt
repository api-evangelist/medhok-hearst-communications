# MHK (formerly MedHOK)

> MHK, part of Hearst Health, is a healthcare technology company providing a unified SaaS platform for health plans and payers. Its CareProminence and MarketProminence suites cover utilization management, care management, complaints/appeals and grievances, pharmacy management, population health and quality, interoperability, enrollment, premium billing, and financial reconciliation. MHK does not operate a public developer portal or API program; this file is generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from public pages.
