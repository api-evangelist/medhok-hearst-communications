Medicare Advantage quality has always carried financial consequences. In 2026, those consequences are especially difficult to ignore. Federal spending through the Medicare Advantage Quality Bonus Program (QBP) is expected to reach at least $13.4 billion this year, according to a recent Kaiser Family Foundation (KFF) analysis of CMS data.
